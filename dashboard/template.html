Metric definitions and limits

Retained order value: item sale value excluding cancelled items and subtracting currently returned items. This view groups items by their creation date and current source state; it can be restated when older returns arrive. It is neither settled cash nor GAAP revenue.

Attainment: sum of retained value for buckets with a declared plan divided by sum of those targets. Unplanned buckets are excluded from this ratio and reported explicitly. A declared zero plan contributes its value to the planned aggregate, but its individual ratio is unavailable. Do not average row percentages.

Fiscal convention: February starts a fiscal year named by its ending year. This original demonstration uses calendar months, not an employer calendar or a 4-4-5 schedule.

Prices and current costs are rounded to integer cents per item before aggregation. Null departments remain visible as Unmapped. No forecasts, causal lift, historical accounting cost or employer performance are claimed.
